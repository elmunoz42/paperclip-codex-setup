# Editorial standard

Every material factual claim needs a primary-source URL where available, publisher, publication/access date, and claim-specific support. Distinguish verified fact, analysis, proposal, and open question. A source ledger is not itself an independent fact-check. Chief of Staff checks metadata and material claims before the consolidated Carlos review. Keep uncertainty in review notes; use natural spoken prose in scripts. Carlos approves editorial direction and final public output. Draft acceptance does not authorize publication.
