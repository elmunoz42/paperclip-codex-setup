# First two-week backlog

## Week 1 — establish and run the thin slice

1. **Choose pilot topic and companion format** — Carlos; 20 minutes. Output: topic brief with target audience and decision date.
2. **Build source ledger** — Research & Editorial Producer; one working day. Output: 5–10 sourced claims, uncertainties, and proposed angle.
3. **Draft review-ready package** — Research & Editorial Producer with Chief of Staff QA; two working days. Output: script and article/notebook outline.
4. **Editorial review** — Carlos; 30–60 minutes. Output: approve/revise/kill decision and reusable feedback rubric.
5. **Record operational baseline** — Chief of Staff; 30 minutes. Output: Paperclip health, memory/swap observation, and no-change systemd readiness check.

## Week 2 — turn feedback into a repeatable unit

6. **Revise pilot and define handoff checklist** — Chief of Staff + Producer. Output: final internal package and reusable checklist; do not publish.
7. **Map Math for Programmers pilot** — Carlos + Chief of Staff. Output: learner promise and 4–6 lesson sequence; select one pilot lesson.
8. **Prepare second topic brief** — Carlos + Producer. Output: next research assignment using the new rubric.
9. **Request platform discovery inputs** — Chief of Staff. Output: named repositories/access paths and desired audit questions for WordPress and immersive app.
10. **Review systemd migration runbook** — Carlos. Output: explicit go/no-go for a later maintenance window; no activation in this sprint.

## Definition of sprint success

One cited, review-ready content package exists; Carlos has reviewed it; feedback has been captured; and no production system has changed.
