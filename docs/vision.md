# Vision

Space Exploration News is Carlos Munoz Kampff's media and product ecosystem for explaining space exploration, mathematics, programming, technology, and AI/ML with rigor and curiosity.

## North star

Build a trustworthy, repeatable content engine that turns primary-source research into compelling YouTube programming and useful companion learning material—without displacing Carlos's editorial judgment or on-camera perspective.

## Audience and value

- Curious technologists who want current space news with context.
- Programmers learning the mathematics behind graphics, simulation, data, and space.
- Learners exploring AI/ML, missions, careers, and the space economy.

The differentiator is not volume. It is clear explanations that join space reporting to practical technical learning.

## Principles

1. Primary sources before commentary; attribution stays attached to each claim.
2. One strong episode can produce several coherent supporting assets.
3. Validate one repeatable workflow before adding agent roles or platform automation.
4. Existing WordPress and immersive-site investments are separate product surfaces, not reasons to duplicate or replace systems.
5. Human review gates public output and material editorial decisions.
