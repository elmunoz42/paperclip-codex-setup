# Content engine: first thin slice

## Outcome

For one Carlos-approved topic, produce a review-ready YouTube script plus either a companion article outline or a Python-notebook outline. This is a draft package, not a publishing workflow.

## Workflow and quality gates

| Stage | Owner | Input | Output | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Topic brief | Carlos | Candidate topic | Audience, angle, format, timely date, companion format | Carlos approves scope |
| 2. Source ledger | Research/editorial agent | Topic brief | 5–10 sources, including primary sources where available; claim/source map | Every material fact attributable |
| 3. Angle & outline | Chief of Staff | Source ledger | Hook, audience promise, thesis, beats, caveats | Separate fact from analysis |
| 4. Script draft | Research/editorial agent | Approved outline | Spoken-word draft with on-screen/source cues | Carlos reviews tone and claims |
| 5. Companion outline | Same agent | Script and ledger | Article or notebook structure, links, definitions, CTA placeholder | No unsupported expansion |
| 6. Review packet | Chief of Staff | All above | One package: summary, open questions, approvals requested | Carlos accepts/revises; no auto-publish |

## Source ledger format

| Claim ID | Statement | Classification | Source | Publisher/date | Accessed | Confidence/notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-01 | … | Verified fact / Analysis / Proposal | Canonical URL | … | YYYY-MM-DD | Scope, uncertainty, conflicts |

`Verified fact` must trace to a credible source. `Analysis` is the creator's interpretation and must state its reasoning. `Proposal` is a future-facing suggestion. Quotes and figures require exact source locations when practical.

## Smallest initial roster

| Role | Responsibility | Inputs → outputs | Approval boundary | Completion criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chief of Staff (current) | Prioritization, workflow quality, decision records | Strategy and research → scoped briefs, review packets, roadmap | Cannot approve editorial/public/production decisions | Package is coherent, traceable, and has a clear human review action |
| Research & Editorial Producer | Source research, claim ledger, outline/script and companion draft | Approved topic → cited research package and drafts | Cannot publish, state analysis as fact, or choose commercial/editorial commitments | Every material fact is traceable; draft meets brief |

Add a third role only after one thin slice: either a Learning Product Designer for repeated Math-course work or a Platform/Product Auditor after app/CMS access is provided. Carlos remains the editor, presenter, and final approver.

## Review packet template

1. Topic, audience, and the requested decision.
2. One-paragraph angle and why it matters now.
3. Source ledger and unresolved/conflicting evidence.
4. Script draft with verified-fact/analysis distinctions visible in notes.
5. Companion article or notebook outline.
6. Recording requirements and non-publishing next step.
