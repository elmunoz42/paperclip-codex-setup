# SPA-9 onboarding and validation cycle

**Intended audience:** Carlos and the Research & Editorial Producer\
**Status:** Proposal for Carlos review — not for publication\
**Prepared:** 2026-09-05\
**Source material:** [Content engine](docs/content-engine.md), [approved trial packet](docs/trials/research-editorial-producer-trial-packet.md), and the SPA-8 hire decision (94/100; hire with revisions).\
**Review action:** Carlos approves or revises the first thin-slice topic and angle. Production starts only after that gate is recorded.

## Outcome

Validate the hired Research & Editorial Producer through three offline, review-ready draft packages while keeping all editorial and publication authority with Carlos. The cycle ends with an evidence-backed recommendation to retain the role as scoped, revise it, or investigate a repeatable specialist need.

## Onboarding standard

The producer receives these non-negotiable rules before assignment:

- Every material factual claim must map to a claim-level source record containing a canonical URL, publisher, publication date, access date, classification, and a note stating exactly what the source supports and does not support.
- Use the labels **verified fact**, **analysis**, **proposal**, and **open question**. Interpretation must never be presented as sourced fact.
- Scholarly or technical bibliographic metadata receives a second-person check by the Chief of Staff before Carlos reviews the package.
- Governance language must describe only the workflow directly established by sources. Broad phrases such as “humans retained mission control” are not acceptable unless the cited evidence supports that exact scope.
- Conflicts, uncertainty, missing evidence, copyright/asset questions, and risk-sensitive wording stay visible.
- The producer may research, organize, draft, and revise offline. The role may not publish, contact sources, access production systems, incur spend, choose commercial commitments, or approve its own editorial angle or risk-sensitive claims.
- Every artifact must say **Draft for Carlos review — not for publication**.

## Proposed package sequence and gates

### Package 1 — End-to-end thin slice

**Proposal:** Use the already researched Mars-rover angle: “When a Mars rover uses generative AI to plan a drive, what is the AI deciding—and what remains under human control?” The focus remains a cautious explanation of waypoint planning, navigation, autonomy, and documented human workflow—not a claim of unsupervised operation.

Deliverables: topic brief, 5–10-source ledger, outline, 700–1,000-word spoken draft, companion article or teaching-notebook outline, revision note, and unresolved-question register.

Gates:

1. Carlos approves or revises topic, angle, audience, and companion format.
2. Chief of Staff checks ledger completeness, metadata, claim mapping, and fact/analysis separation.
3. Carlos reviews outline, tone, factual/risk-sensitive claims, and revision direction.
4. Producer revises; Chief of Staff assembles the review packet. No publication follows from acceptance.

### Package 2 — Additional AI/space crossover

The Chief of Staff will present a source-backed topic brief after Package 1 feedback. Recommended starting candidate: NASA/IBM Prithvi geospatial foundation-model activity in orbit, framed around what on-orbit inference can and cannot establish.

The same ledger, metadata, classification, review, and non-publication gates apply.

### Package 3 — General space news

The Chief of Staff will present a timely, primary-source-led general space-news brief after Package 2 feedback. The final topic must not depend on AI as its core angle.

The same ledger, metadata, classification, review, and non-publication gates apply.

## Validation record

For each package, the Chief of Staff records:

- deliverables received and word-count/format checks;
- every material claim’s ledger mapping;
- bibliographic metadata exceptions and corrections;
- unsupported, overbroad, conflicting, or risk-sensitive claims;
- Carlos’s decision at each required gate, with revision instructions;
- whether the producer stayed within role boundaries;
- repeatable work that may justify a specialist role later.

Any fabricated citation, invented quote or figure, material undisclosed unsupported claim, or representation that a draft is publishable is a stop condition and must be escalated to Carlos.

## Day-30 review

The review packet will contain:

1. recommendation: **retain as scoped**, **revise the role**, or **investigate a repeatable specialist need**;
2. evidence from all three packages and Carlos’s recorded gate decisions;
3. assumptions and unresolved questions;
4. source-discipline and boundary-adherence findings;
5. quality or workload patterns that repeated across packages;
6. one clear action requested from Carlos.

## Assumptions and open questions

**Assumption:** The first package can reuse the trial’s Mars AI-planning topic without weakening the validation, because SPA-9 tests an end-to-end full package and revision cycle rather than repeating the short trial excerpt.

**Open question:** Carlos must approve or revise the proposed Package 1 topic and cautious angle before the producer receives the production assignment.

**Open question:** Carlos may choose an article outline or a teaching-notebook outline as the companion format at the first gate.
