## Editorial acceptance rubric

- Opening minute names a concrete question, equation, runnable artifact, and experiment the viewer will understand.
- Identify the actual mission algorithm, where it runs, and what the public evidence establishes. Separate learned models from model-based robotics.
- Connect every equation to units, assumptions, code, and an observable result. Explain one failure case.
- Label historical flight software, ground experiments, research prototypes, and teaching approximations separately.
- Provide reproducible instructions and an independent correctness check. A polished interface alone does not meet the bar.
- End with what Carlos learned, what remains uncertain, and one useful experiment the reader can change.
